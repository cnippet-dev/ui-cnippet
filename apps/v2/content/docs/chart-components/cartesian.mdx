---
title: Cartesian
description: Charts on x/y axes for trends, comparisons and correlations.
---

Cartesian charts cover most dashboards: trends over time, comparisons between categories and how two numbers relate. They share the [common chart props](/docs/chart-components/foundation#common-props), so switching from an area chart to a bar chart is usually a one-word change.

- **[Area Chart](/charts/cartesian/area-chart)** — Trends over time; default, stacked or percent.
- **[Bar Chart](/charts/cartesian/bar-chart)** — Comparisons; columns or horizontal bars, grouped, stacked or percent.
- **[Line Chart](/charts/cartesian/line-chart)** — Trends with dots, dashed forecasts and gap handling.
- **[Combo Chart](/charts/cartesian/combo-chart)** — Bars and lines together, with an optional second axis.
- **[Scatter Chart](/charts/cartesian/scatter-chart)** — Correlations between two numbers, with bubbles and groups.
