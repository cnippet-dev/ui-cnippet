---
title: Sparklines & Micro
description: Small, label-free charts for KPI cards, tables and lists.
---

Micro charts sit next to a number and show its shape: a trend, a share, progress or a status history. Most are plain HTML, CSS or SVG, so they're cheap enough to put in every row of a table.

- **[Spark Area](/charts/micro/spark-area)** — Tiny area trend, coloured by direction.
- **[Spark Line](/charts/micro/spark-line)** — Tiny line trend with an optional last-point dot.
- **[Spark Bar](/charts/micro/spark-bar)** — Tiny bars for volume or win/loss.
- **[Bar List](/charts/micro/bar-list)** — Ranked bars with a name and value per row.
- **[Category Bar](/charts/micro/category-bar)** — One bar split into ranges, with a marker.
- **[Progress Circle](/charts/micro/progress-circle)** — Ring showing progress toward a goal.
- **[Delta Bar](/charts/micro/delta-bar)** — Change growing left or right from the centre.
- **[Tracker](/charts/micro/tracker)** — Row of status blocks with tooltips.
- **[Bullet Chart](/charts/micro/bullet-chart)** — Value against a target over qualitative ranges.
