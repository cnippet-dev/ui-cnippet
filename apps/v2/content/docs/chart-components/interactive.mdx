---
title: Interactive & Real-time
description: Charts that respond to the user or to live data.
---

These charts add a way to explore: change the range, zoom, switch metrics, drill in, or watch data arrive. Most wrap the [Cartesian](/docs/chart-components/cartesian) charts, so they take the same [common props](/docs/chart-components/foundation#common-props).

- **[Time Range Chart](/charts/interactive/time-range-chart)** — 7d / 30d / 90d toggle with totals for the range.
- **[Brush Chart](/charts/interactive/brush-chart)** — Zoom and pan a long series with a brush.
- **[Series Toggle Chart](/charts/interactive/series-toggle-chart)** — Metric tabs that switch the series shown.
- **[Synced Charts](/charts/interactive/synced-charts)** — Stacked panels sharing one cursor.
- **[Realtime Chart](/charts/interactive/realtime-chart)** — Streaming line with a pause button.
- **[Drill-down Chart](/charts/interactive/drilldown-chart)** — Click or press Enter on a bar to open its breakdown.
- **[Annotated Chart](/charts/interactive/annotated-chart)** — Event markers, targets and shaded periods.
