---
title: Hierarchy & Flow
description: Charts for how a total splits into parts and how quantities move between stages.
---

These charts answer "where does it go?" A funnel follows one group through ordered steps, a treemap breaks a total into nested parts, and a sankey follows flows that split and merge between stages.

- **[Funnel Chart](/charts/hierarchy/funnel-chart)** — Ordered steps with the share reaching each one and the drop-off between them.
- **[Treemap](/charts/hierarchy/treemap)** — A total broken into parts and sub-parts, sized by value.
- **[Sankey](/charts/hierarchy/sankey)** — Flows between named stages, with link widths proportional to value.
