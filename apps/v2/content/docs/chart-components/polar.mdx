---
title: Circular & Polar
description: Charts for part-to-whole, scores and progress toward a goal.
---

Circular charts answer "what share?" and "how close?". Use them for a handful of parts or a single score; for comparing many values, a bar chart is easier to read.

- **[Donut Chart](/charts/polar/donut-chart)** — Part-to-whole ring with a centre value.
- **[Pie Chart](/charts/polar/pie-chart)** — Part-to-whole pie; a donut with no hole.
- **[Radar Chart](/charts/polar/radar-chart)** — Several series compared across many axes.
- **[Radial Chart](/charts/polar/radial-chart)** — Concentric progress rings.
- **[Gauge Chart](/charts/polar/gauge-chart)** — Half-circle gauge with threshold bands and a needle.
- **[Sunburst Chart](/charts/polar/sunburst-chart)** — Two nested rings: categories and their breakdown.
- **[Polar Area Chart](/charts/polar/polar-area-chart)** — Rose chart for cyclic data like weekdays or directions.
