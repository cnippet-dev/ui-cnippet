---
title: Layout
description: Structural components for composing surfaces, spacing, and scrollable regions.
---

Layout components provide the structural building blocks — surfaces, grouping, spacing, and scroll containment.

- **[Card](/ui/layout/card)** — A container for grouping related content and actions.
- **[Frame](/ui/layout/frame)** — A bordered surface for framing content, such as a preview or demo.
- **[Group](/ui/layout/group)** — Visually groups a set of related elements together.
- **[Preview Card](/ui/layout/preview-card)** — Shows a preview of content when hovering over a trigger.
- **[Scroll Area](/ui/layout/scroll-area)** — A container with a custom, cross-browser scrollbar.
- **[Separator](/ui/layout/separator)** — Visually or semantically separates content.
