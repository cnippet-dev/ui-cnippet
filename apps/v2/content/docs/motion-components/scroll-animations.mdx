---
title: Scroll Animations
description: Components that animate content in response to scroll position.
---

Scroll animation components tie motion to scroll progress — revealing, tracking, or transforming content as the user scrolls.

- **[Scroll Reveal](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-reveal)** — Reveals content as it enters the viewport while scrolling.
- **[Scroll Progress](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-progress)** — A progress indicator that tracks scroll position.
- **[Scroll Velocity Text](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-velocity-text)** — Text that moves based on scroll speed and direction.
- **[Parallax Floating](/motion/scroll-animations/parallax-floating)** — Elements that drift at different speeds to create a parallax effect.
- **[Stacking Cards](/motion/scroll-animations/stacking-cards)** — Cards that stack on top of each other as the user scrolls.
- **[Progressive Blur](/motion/scroll-animations/progressive-blur)** — Applies a gradient blur that intensifies with scroll.
- **[Sticky Scroll Reveal](/motion/scroll-animations/sticky-scroll-reveal)** — Text steps scroll past a sticky panel that swaps to each step's content.
- **[Container Scroll](/motion/scroll-animations/container-scroll)** — A card tilts flat and settles as it scrolls into view.
- **[Hero Parallax](/motion/scroll-animations/hero-parallax)** — Rows of product cards slide sideways in opposite directions on scroll.
- **[Scroll Image Reveal](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-image-reveal)** — Reveals an image with a clip-path wipe or circle on scroll.
- **[Macbook Scroll](/motion/scroll-animations/macbook-scroll)** — A laptop lid opens on scroll to reveal your screen content.
- **[Horizontal Scroll](/motion/scroll-animations/horizontal-scroll)** — Pins a section and turns vertical scrolling into horizontal movement.
- **[Scroll Direction Header](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-direction-header)** — A header that hides while scrolling down and returns on scroll up.
- **[Sticky Footer](/motion/scroll-animations/sticky-footer)** — A footer uncovered beneath the page at the end of the scroll.
- **[Scroll Snap Sections](/motion/scroll-animations/scroll-snap-sections)** — Full-height sections that snap into place, with dot navigation.
