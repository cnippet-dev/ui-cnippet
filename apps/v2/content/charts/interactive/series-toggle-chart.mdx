## Good to know

- One series shows at a time, so series with very different scales (users vs revenue) each get a full y-axis.
- Each tab shows the series' total over all rows. For rates and averages, turn totals off with `showTotals={false}`.
- `formatters` sets a formatter per series, used on its tab and on the chart when it's selected, e.g. `{ revenue: formatCurrency }`.
- Tabs are buttons with `aria-pressed`, in a group labelled "Metric".
- To show or hide several series at once instead, use the [Interactive Legend](/charts/foundation/interactive-legend).

## Props

Takes all the [common chart props](/docs/chart-components/foundation#common-props), plus:

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `chartType` | `"area" \| "bar" \| "line"` | `"bar"` | Chart to draw |
| `defaultCategory` | `keyof T` | first category | Series shown at first |
| `onCategoryChange` | `(category) => void` | — | Called when a tab is chosen |
| `showTotals` | `boolean` | `true` | Show totals on the tabs |
| `formatters` | `Record<category, (value: number) => string>` | — | Per-series formatters |
